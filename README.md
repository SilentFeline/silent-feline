### `whoami`

I investigate what hides in files, logs, and network traffic. From malware artifacts to threat intelligence, I turn scattered signals into a clearer picture of the threat. From uncovering the story behind a cyber threat to pulling apart a suspicious file, I'm drawn to the questions that don't have obvious answers.

Quietly curious. Constantly investigating. Some things are worth looking at twice.

```bash
$ mission.txt
Understand the behavior.
Follow the evidence.
Turn findings into detections.
```
